Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

Neff.io is a personal project operated by Steve Neff. It hosts a small number of private, invitation-only tools alongside some freely readable publications. This policy describes what happens to your information when you sign in to one of the private tools.

Who this applies to

The access-controlled services at service.neffio.com, automate.neffio.com and neffio.com/todoist are available only to a short list of named individuals who were invited directly. There is no public sign-up. If you have not been given access by Steve Neff, none of this applies to you.

The public parts of this site — the landing page, the Excel Tips Weekly Digest and the PM Excelerated Codex — require no sign-in and collect nothing from you.

What is collected when you sign in

Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access, which sits in front of these tools and checks your identity before any request reaches the server. You may sign in either with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address.

The only piece of information used to make the access decision is your email address, which is compared against a fixed list. If it is on the list you are let through; if it is not, you are refused. When you sign in with Google, Google also supplies your basic profile details (your name and profile picture) as part of the standard sign-in response.

Signing in here does not give these tools access to your Google account. The sign-in requests only your identity — your email address and basic profile. It does not request, and cannot obtain, access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.

What is stored

  • A session cookie in your browser, set by Cloudflare, so you are not asked to sign in again on every page. You can end a session at any time by visiting /cdn-cgi/access/logout on the relevant subdomain.
  • Sign-in records kept by Cloudflare, which log the fact that an authentication happened — typically the email address used, a timestamp and the originating IP address. These are retained by Cloudflare under its own retention schedule and are used only to see who accessed what, and to investigate problems.
  • Your email address on the access list itself, which is stored in the Cloudflare configuration for as long as you have access.

Your Google profile details are not copied into a database of our own. Some of the tools do store the working content you create while using them — for example service plans entered into the Service Director — and that content is kept for as long as the tool is in use.

What is not done

  • Your information is never sold, rented or traded.
  • It is not shared with anyone beyond the service providers described below.
  • It is not used to advertise to you or to build a profile of you.
  • These pages carry no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking scripts.

Service providers

Two third parties necessarily handle information in the course of signing you in: Cloudflare, which performs the authentication and carries the traffic, and Google, if you choose Google as your sign-in method. Each handles your data under its own privacy policy. Individual tools may rely on further services to do their job, using credentials belonging to the site operator rather than to you.

Your choices

You can use the one-time email code instead of Google if you would prefer not to involve your Google account. You can ask to have your access removed at any time, which deletes your email address from the access list. You can also ask what is held about you. For any of these, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Because the audience is a handful of people, material changes will simply be mentioned directly.
