Using ABS and SIGN Functions for Robust Financial Calculations and Logical Operations

This email demonstrates practical applications of underrated Excel functions ABS and SIGN. ABS eliminates sign from calculations to correctly handle percentage changes with negative values and validate tolerance ranges; SIGN converts numeric values to -1, 0, or 1 to enable efficient OR logic within SUMPRODUCT without double-counting. Both solve common business problems across all Excel versions.
