ABS and SIGN functions for robust financial calculations and logical testing

ABS removes sign information to correctly handle percentage change calculations with negative values and simplify variance tolerance checks. SIGN converts numbers to -1/0/1 for building OR logic within SUMPRODUCT to avoid double-counting when multiple conditions are true.
