Using ABS and SIGN Functions for Financial Calculations and Logical Operations

ABS removes sign information to focus on magnitude, solving problems like year-on-year percentage changes with negative values and invoice tolerance checks. SIGN converts numbers to -1, 0, or 1, enabling efficient OR logic within SUMPRODUCT to avoid double-counting when multiple conditions are true.
