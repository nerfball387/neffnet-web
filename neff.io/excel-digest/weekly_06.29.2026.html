Cell Styles for Workbook Consistency and Readability

Cell Styles provide a standardized way to visually distinguish input cells from calculated cells across an entire workbook, ensuring consistency and reducing formula errors. Users can modify built-in styles (input, calculated, hyperlink) or create custom styles with specific formatting (fill, font, border), then apply them globally rather than formatting individual cells.
