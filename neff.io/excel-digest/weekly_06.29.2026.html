Copilot Personalization in Excel

Copilot in Excel now includes a Personalization feature that learns and remembers user preferences (table formatting, cell formats, function choices) across workbooks and devices, eliminating the need to repeat detailed specifications in every prompt. This reduces prompt length and ensures consistent output formatting without manual repetition.
