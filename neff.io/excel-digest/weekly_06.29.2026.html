Combining IF with AND/OR for multi-condition logic

This technique demonstrates nesting AND and OR functions within IF statements to evaluate multiple conditions simultaneously. AND requires all conditions to be true; OR requires only one. The approach enables approval logic, classifications, and decision rules that would otherwise require nested IF statements or manual review.
