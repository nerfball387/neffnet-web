Combining IF with AND/OR for multi-condition logic

This technique demonstrates nesting AND and OR functions within IF statements to evaluate multiple conditions simultaneously. AND requires all conditions to be true; OR requires at least one. Common applications include approval workflows, claim processing, and decision rules that would otherwise require nested IFs or manual review.

Advanced Find and Replace with Format Matching and Wildcards

Excel's Find and Replace dialog offers advanced options beyond text substitution, including format-only replacement, wildcard pattern matching, formula vs. value targeting, and workbook-wide operations. The Options panel reveals capabilities like replacing number formatting independently of cell values, searching within formulas or comments, and removing invisible characters like line breaks.
