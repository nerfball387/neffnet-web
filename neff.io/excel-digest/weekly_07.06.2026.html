Using the N() Function to Embed Formula Comments

The N() function converts text to zero, allowing explanatory comments to be embedded directly within formulas without altering their results. This technique makes complex formulas self-documenting and auditable by keeping business logic and explanatory text together in the formula bar, particularly useful for formulas containing business rules, thresholds, or assumptions.
